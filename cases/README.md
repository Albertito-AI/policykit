# Worked evidence cases

These examples connect narrow legal propositions to primary sources and exact
pinpoints. They are research records, not assessments of an actual issuer or
evidence of a completed human review.

The [landmark and key case workflow rationale](../docs/CASE_LAW_WORKFLOW.md)
records the collection's ambition, selection criteria, source-to-proposition
method and development priorities. Consult its decision guide when choosing
coverage or resolving uncertainty. Planned capabilities are labelled separately
from the current case records.

| Case | Scope and source date | Review status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Singapore sustainability reporting](singapore-sustainability-reporting/README.md) | SGX Mainboard reporting timing and internal review; official materials consulted on 23 October 2026 | AI-prepared; both propositions `unreviewed`; independent human review outstanding |
| [Singapore and Thailand, 2023 reporting](singapore-thailand-disclosure-2023/README.md) | Two matched questions on sustainability targets and stakeholder reporting, using the historical SGX Mainboard instruments and the Thai SEC’s 2023 standard form | Four AI-prepared propositions open to independent review |
| [Singapore and Thailand, 2023 and 2025 reporting years](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/sg-th-disclosure-data) | Eight observations on targets and stakeholder reporting, with version-specific sources and a table comparing years | Two separate AI readings of the 2025 sources, with independent human review pending |

Each case records its jurisdiction, source versions, qualifications and unresolved
questions. The individual worked cases use JSON ledgers. The linked two-year dataset provides a codebook and three CSV tables in its own repository. Structural validation does not establish legal accuracy,
temporal applicability or the competence of a reviewer.

See the [research standard](../docs/RESEARCH_STANDARD.md),
[schema](../docs/schema.md) and [rights statement](../RIGHTS.md). These additions
do not grant a reuse licence or change third-party source terms.
