# Worked evidence cases

These examples connect narrow legal propositions to primary sources and exact
pinpoints. They are research records, not assessments of an actual issuer or
evidence of a completed human review.

| Case | Scope and source date | Review status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Singapore sustainability reporting](singapore-sustainability-reporting/README.md) | SGX Mainboard reporting timing and internal review; official materials consulted on 23 October 2026 | AI-prepared; both propositions `unreviewed`; independent human review outstanding |

Each case records its jurisdiction, source versions, qualifications and unresolved
questions. The JSON ledger is the complete record; a generated matrix is a view
of those entries. Structural validation does not establish legal accuracy,
temporal applicability or the competence of a reviewer.

See the [research standard](../docs/RESEARCH_STANDARD.md),
[schema](../docs/schema.md) and [rights statement](../RIGHTS.md). These additions
do not grant a reuse licence or change third-party source terms.
