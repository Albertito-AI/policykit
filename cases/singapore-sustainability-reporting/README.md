# Singapore sustainability reporting: a narrow evidence case

**AI-prepared example. Both propositions remain `unreviewed`. Independent human
review is outstanding.** No reviewer, maintainer endorsement or issuer-specific
compliance finding is asserted.

## Question and scope

How do SGX Mainboard provisions distinguish the annual sustainability-report
timetable, internal review of the reporting process and additional external
assurance, without mistaking that timetable for a blanket postponement of
separately triggered market disclosures under Rule 703?

The [ledger](ledger.json) contains two modest textual propositions, identified as
`sgx-mb-report-timing` and `sgx-mb-internal-review`. It uses real primary sources;
there is no fictional issuer, alleged breach or adjudicated dispute. The example
is limited to the versions SGX displayed as applicable from 1 January 2026,
consulted on **23 October 2026**. Applicability to a particular issuer, reporting
period, waiver or listing history has not been determined.

Climate-disclosure phasing, IFRS/ISSB requirements, Catalist rules, statutory
causes of action, liability, sanctions and remedies are outside this case. The
Rule 703 link is a **narrow qualification**, not an issuer-specific finding
that a market announcement was required, omitted or late.

### What source verification establishes

The dated SGX version labels for Rules 711A, 711B and Practice Note 7.6 were
confirmed from current official HTML. Rule 703 was read directly in the
**Mainboard** section, not inferred from the similarly numbered Catalist rule.
The 23 October amendment PDF is a redline: deleted and inserted text must not
be combined into a purported current rule. It was not newly checked on
26 October. Practice Note 7.6 paragraph 1.2 expressly preserves any distinct
market disclosure duty under Rule 703.

This is a documented AI source-inspection pass, not independent human legal
verification. The effective date comes from SGX's labels and amendment heading;
it is distinct from the access date. A separate publication date has not been
established. The live URLs may change, and this case does not preserve a licensed
full-text snapshot or certify later changes. Its `as_of` metadata does not itself
prove that a provision applies to a particular report.

## Evidence relationships and limits

- **Timing and continuing disclosure:** Rule 711A supports the ordinary annual
  report-timing proposition and qualifies it with an external-assurance
  alternative. Practice Note 7.6 paragraph 6.1 describes that alternative as
  transitional; paragraph 6.2 requires an annual-report summary when the
  sustainability report follows it. Practice Note 7.6 paragraph 1.2 and
  Mainboard Rule 703(1) distinguish any separately triggered market announcement.
  Rule 703(2)-(3) supply relevant exceptions; no facts are supplied to decide
  whether an announcement was due or an exception was available.
- **Review and evidence of preparation:** Rule 711B(3)'s first sentence
  supports internal review of the reporting **process**. Its second sentence
  allows additional independent external assurance of the **report**. Practice
  Note 7.6 paragraph 5.3 allows a risk-based internal review to consider already
  assured aspects. Neither an apparently accurate report nor a third-party
  assurance label, on its own, records that an issuer actually completed the
  internal-review process or made the relevant risk-based decision.
- **Version:** The amendment document is linked as `context` for the stated
  version, not as a separate legal conclusion about a particular issuer.

No contrary authority is recorded in this bounded pass. That does not establish
that none exists. An independent human reviewer should read each provision in
context, confirm the relevant version and reporting period, check any applicable
waiver or newly listed issuer treatment, and assess each relationship before
entering a review declaration. No completed human review has been recorded.

## Issuer- and statement-specific review gate

The ledger has **two unreviewed propositions**, both about SGX listing rules.
The following is a reviewer intake guide, **not** a third finding or an
assessment of an actual issuer. It operationalises the distinction between the
reporting obligation, the scope of the reported information and possible legal
consequences. The analytical distinction is discussed in
[Tan, *Converging Standards, Divergent Accountability* (2026)](https://research.rug.nl/en/publications/converging-standards-divergent-accountability-sustainability-repo/),
an academic framework with a **30 July 2026** legal cut-off. The official SGX
provisions linked here supply the present case's legal reference points.

**Fictional teaching contrast (no decided facts):** a report might be factually
accurate while evidence of the required internal review is missing. That is an
**evidence gap**, not proof of non-performance. Separately, a permitted later
annual report date would not settle whether a distinct known development
required timely disclosure under Rule 703. Only an appropriately qualified
reviewer using the applicable instruments and contemporaneous records could
reach a fact-specific conclusion. No expert reference decision has been
supplied or simulated as a completed review.

## Reproduce the structural checks

From the repository root:

```sh
python -m regnav_evidence validate cases/singapore-sustainability-reporting/ledger.json
python -m regnav_evidence report cases/singapore-sustainability-reporting/ledger.json --format markdown
```

These commands run offline and do not fetch or verify source URLs. The expected
review-gap warnings are deliberate: `unreviewed` must remain visible until a
human review actually occurs. The report is emitted to standard output; the
JSON remains the complete record, including source notes and access dates.
