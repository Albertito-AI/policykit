### Refined causal chain for the wholly fictional Issuer A example

This is a **proposed review method, not a court outcome**. Before claiming that a sustainability report's inaccurate emissions figure caused Investor B's trading loss or that assurer C must compensate it, identify separately: (1) the actual **defendant and claimant** and whether any contractual relationship exists; (2) which legal wrong is pleaded, including any direct representation to B; (3) the precise communication and B's **actual reliance or other statutory causal route**, not assumed reliance by all market participants; (4) the proposed breach and the acts expected of an otherwise competent professional; (5) a counterfactual with evidence about **what would have changed** and when; (6) other causes of price movement and loss; (7) the legally protected type of loss and **contractual or tortious remoteness**; (8) illegality, contributory fault, exclusion clauses and mitigation where properly available; (9) the remedial claimant, quantification and no double recovery. The investor, auditor and issuer are different actors. Do not treat an auditor-company duty as proof of an investor-facing duty.

For a **contractual assurance** inquiry, require the actual engagement letter, addressees, intended users, reliance restrictions, assurance standard and version, specified reporting boundary, period, performed procedures and contemporaneous communications. Also establish who accepted the contract and whether any recognised legal basis gives an outsider enforceable rights. **No such contract or relevant completed sustainability-assurance adjudication has been inspected.** The statutory-audit judgments provide *issue prompts*, not an adjudication of ESG assurance obligations.

### Two distinct legal gateways, rather than an assumed investor remedy

1. **Contractual enforcement by a non-party:** obtain the actual engagement letter, terms of reference, assurance statement, who signed, governing law, scope, identified beneficiaries, express enforcement language, intended users, exclusions, reliance limits and material period. Apply the period-matched third-party-rights statute and contractual construction, including potentially contradictory terms. A report being published to investors does **not** establish that every investor is an intended contractual beneficiary. *CLAAS* is authority for a method of construction, not an assurance holding.
2. **Independent tortious responsibility:** identify the actual defendant, claimant and publication/communication, apply *Spandeck* to foreseeability, proximity and policy, and examine any *actual* assumption of responsibility or reliance. The engagement's restrictions may be relevant evidence, but the Court of Appeal in *Tan Woo Thian* **left the professional fact-finder's non-client duty open**. A private engagement clause is not automatically a defence binding outsiders.
3. **Existence of caused loss before quantum:** for the wholly fictional Investor B, identify whether any alleged share-price decline occurred and what evidence links it to the specific representation, correction or report, after considering other causes and B's actual reliance where required. *Tan Woo Thian* [2021] [6]–[12] makes proof of causally connected damage indispensable even if the precise amount is reserved for a later stage. It does not decide the separate reflective-loss, securities-statute or contractual-remoteness inquiries.
4. **Assurance specificity:** statutory audits, investigative findings and sustainability-assurance opinions have different mandates, beneficiaries, standards, levels of assurance and professional duties. None of these cases resolves the actual terms or standard of an ESG assurance engagement. Identify the contract, applicable standard and any legislative mandate before moving from analogy to an issuer-specific proposition.
