## External evidence baseline

No independent pilot observations, verified external research use or external
legal-review declaration have been recorded in this project at this baseline.
That is a statement about the available project record, not a claim that no
one has privately viewed or used the repository. Traffic and downstream use
are not inferred from the absence of public issues.

The public work so far is maintainer-directed and AI-assisted. Internal
cross-checks, commits, pull requests and scheduled executions are development
activity; they are not independent adoption or scholarly endorsement.
Related publications listed in the README establish intellectual context,
not validation of this software.

## Evidence to collect next

| Question | Useful evidence | Reporting limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can someone complete the documented workflow? | Actual observations from the prepared pilot, including help needed and obstacles | Report the small sample and task conditions; do not infer a general benefit |
| Are the two legal propositions adequately supported? | A real person's examination of the identified source passages, scope and qualifications | Record the reviewer and limits only after review actually occurs |
| Does the project support another research or teaching task? | A verifiable public use, citation or an account shared with permission | Distinguish independent work from the maintainer's own demonstration |
| Does maintenance improve use? | A reproducible report, a specific correction and a follow-up observation where available | Closing an issue alone does not establish that a user's problem was solved |

For each new observation, retain the date, source, project version, relationship
to the maintainer, basis of the observation and appropriate permission to
publish it. Keep raw participant forms and private correspondence outside
this repository. A public summary should be accurate, limited and anonymised
where needed.

Stars and forks can describe visible attention at a particular moment, but
they do not demonstrate research quality, learning or useful adoption.
Prioritise the concrete evidence above when deciding what to build next.
