# Descriptive rubric for the fictional exercise

**Status:** unvalidated teaching rubric, developed with AI assistance. It
describes work on this source packet only. No pass mark, learning gain or
general legal competence is established by a score.

Use alongside the [answer key](facilitator_guide.md). Award 0, 1 or 2 in each
row and quote or paraphrase the part of the learner's response which supports
the score. Leave a row unscored if the task was not attempted or observed;
do not silently treat missing data as a zero. Discuss the profile of responses
before considering a total. If all six rows are observed, the possible total
is 0 to 12.

| Criterion | 0: not demonstrated in the response | 1: partly demonstrated | 2: clearly demonstrated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source and pinpoint discipline | Makes substantive assertions without a relevant source location | Gives relevant source names but incomplete or imprecise pinpoints | Uses A.2, A.3 and B.2 for their respective points; inspects the complete packet for the further-review claim |
| Supported logging claim | Infers a sequence for both drafts without evidence | Identifies A.2 but leaves the scope unclear | Limits the sequencing claim to Draft A and explains that B.1 does not specify the sequence |
| Qualification of response time | Omits or changes the unit without explanation | Notices "working" but does not repair the draft consistently | Preserves "five working days" and explains the relation between the original and revised proposition |
| Conflict and governing authority | Suppresses one deadline or selects a governing draft without evidence | Identifies five versus ten working days but does not address precedence | Records both passages and the absence of a supplied governing rule; does not confuse different deadlines with incompatible performance in every case |
| Unsupported further review | Invents an appeal right or concludes that further review is prohibited | Flags missing evidence but leaves the affirmative claim in the replacement paragraph | Removes or bounds the assertion and distinguishes absent support from proof of prohibition |
| Meaning of structural validation | Treats a clean result or `checked` as proof of legal truth | Recognises some limits but attributes source checking or identity verification to the software | Distinguishes fields and declared review from substantive verification; recognises the fictional reviewer and explains why an irrelevant pinpoint can pass a structural check |
