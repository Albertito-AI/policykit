# Rights and reuse

No open-source licence is supplied with this prototype. Its public availability
and citation metadata should not be read as a general licence to reuse it.
Contact the maintainer through the [institutional profile](https://research.rug.nl/en/persons/tan-sin-hon-albert-tan/)
to discuss permissions, collaboration or commercial use.

Any rights statements inside the earlier linked archives remain specific to
those materials. The archives have not been incorporated into this prototype
and this repository does not change their terms. Third-party sources remain
subject to their own terms.

GitHub explains the distinction between a public repository and open-source
licensing in its [licensing documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository).
Future licensing choices should identify the code, original research material
and third-party content separately.
