# Public method and private workspace boundary

## Public repository purpose

RegNav Evidence Ledger is the inspectable research and teaching layer for connecting legal propositions to source records, pinpoints and declared review. Public material may include original code, schemas, public-source worked examples, synthetic training packets, research standards, tests and reproducible reports.

The public repository is not a confidential client file, legal-opinion service, authoritative statement of current law or repository for restricted research material.

## Material that may remain public

- generic evidence-ledger schemas and validators;
- synthetic examples and fictional training sources;
- legal materials already lawfully public, with accurate attribution and pinpoints;
- public research methods, teaching exercises and non-confidential evaluation protocols;
- source-code tests and reproducible demonstrations.

## Material that must remain private

- client instructions, identities, documents, advice and work product;
- unpublished manuscripts, peer-review correspondence and embargoed research;
- interview recordings, transcripts, consent records or participant identifiers;
- paid database exports, restricted publications and copyrighted material not cleared for redistribution;
- credentials, private paths, tokens, local source collections and production databases;
- partner configurations, commercial terms and confidential evaluation results.

## Private workspace trigger

Create a private client or research workspace when a real engagement, restricted source collection, interview programme, unpublished manuscript or confidential evaluation begins. Keep the reusable public method here, but store case-specific content and access records in the restricted workspace.

A public issue may describe a non-confidential problem and synthetic reproduction. It must not contain client documents, legal advice, private source paths, personal data or billing information.

## Release check

Before publication, verify publication rights for every source and quotation, remove personal and confidential information, confirm that source pinpoints and status labels are accurate, and state what remains unreviewed or contested. Removing a file from the current branch does not by itself remove it from Git history.

The repository's `RIGHTS.md` governs reuse. Public visibility alone grants no general licence, legal reliance entitlement, professional engagement or institutional endorsement.
