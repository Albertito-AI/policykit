# Synthetic training sources

These texts are invented solely to demonstrate the software. They are not legal
authorities, institutional policies, legal advice, or descriptions of an actual
jurisdiction. The example.org URLs in the JSON are illustrative. The complete
fixture texts are here; the program does not retrieve a web page.

## Draft A

**A.1** A coordinator receives requests submitted to the fictional project.

**A.2** The coordinator enters each request in a log before a reviewer examines it.

**A.3** The reviewer responds within five working days.

## Draft B

**B.1** A coordinator keeps a register of requests and responses.

**B.2** The reviewer responds within ten working days.

The exercise supplies no rule establishing which draft governs. Neither draft
states that a further review is available after the initial decision. Those
limits deliberately create a contested claim and an unreviewed claim in the
sample ledger. The recorded reviewer and dates are fictional fixtures as well.
